What we collect

When you request a home value report or contact us, we collect what you give us: your name, the property address, your phone number, your email address, and when you are thinking of selling. By submitting the form, you agree that Shlimbaum Realty may call, text or email you about your request at the number and address you gave. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts, or tell us on any call or by email and we will stop contacting you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our customer database, email, phone and website analytics providers, and when the law requires it. If you ask us to refer you to a lender, title company or other professional, we share your details with them only at your request.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to respond to you and to meet our record-keeping duties as a Florida brokerage, then delete it.
