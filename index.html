What is in your home value report?

The report

Your report gives an estimated value range for your address, the closed sales it is based on, and what you would walk away with after costs.

1

An estimated value range

A low and high figure for your home, dated, with the three nearby closed sales behind it. On acreage lots, land size, outbuildings and well and septic condition move the number, so we account for them.

2

A written net sheet

An itemized estimate of what you would keep at closing after commission, title, taxes and your mortgage payoff.

3

A pricing and prep plan

How we would price, prepare and market your home to get the most the market will pay, and what is worth fixing first.

The report is a broker's opinion of value based on public and MLS sales data. It is an estimate and not an appraisal.
